1 What is SEO and why does it matter?

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) is the practice of making your website the most useful, fastest and most trustworthy answer to what people are already searching for. When Google decides a page deserves to rank, it sends free, repeated traffic — every single day — without you paying for each click.

That is why SEO is an asset and advertising is an expense. A paid campaign stops working the moment you stop paying. A well‑optimised page can keep delivering leads for years. For most service businesses, e‑commerce stores and SaaS companies, organic search becomes the single largest and most profitable acquisition channel after 6–12 months of consistent work.

“Rankings are not the goal. Qualified traffic that converts into revenue is the goal — and that is exactly what our SEO strategy is designed around.”

2 The three pillars of SEO

🔍 On‑Page SEO

  • Title tags, meta descriptions & URLs
  • H1–H3 heading structure
  • Keyword placement & readability
  • Image alt text & internal linking
  • Schema / structured data markup

🔗 Off‑Page SEO

  • High‑quality backlink acquisition
  • Digital PR & brand mentions
  • Guest publishing on trusted sites
  • Local citations & directories
  • Review & reputation signals

⚙️ Technical SEO

  • Crawlability, indexation & XML sitemaps
  • Canonical tags, redirects & duplicate control
  • Site architecture & URL hierarchy
  • Mobile‑first indexing & JavaScript rendering
  • Site speed, HTTPS & Core Web Vitals
  • Log analysis, robots.txt & Search Console health

Neglect any one pillar and the other two lose power. A beautiful page with great content will still fail if Google cannot crawl it; a technically perfect site will still fail if nobody links to it or the content does not match search intent. Balance is the strategy.

3 Keyword research & search intent

Before writing a single word, we map keywords by intent, because Google ranks pages that satisfy the reason behind the search:

Intent types we target

  • Informational — “how to improve website speed”
  • Commercial — “best seo agency for small business”
  • Transactional — “hire web developer near me”
  • Navigational — “rankora pricing”

How we choose keywords

  • Search volume vs. realistic difficulty
  • Business value per keyword (not just traffic)
  • Long‑tail clusters that convert faster
  • Competitor gap analysis

We then organise those keywords into topic clusters: one authoritative pillar page supported by many related supporting articles, all internally linked. This is the fastest modern way to build topical authority in a competitive niche.

4 Web development: how your code affects rankings

Search engines are readers of code. A website built with clean, semantic HTML is dramatically easier to understand than one bloated with unnecessary scripts and inline styles. That is why development and SEO are not two departments — they are one discipline.

What we build into every project

  • Semantic HTML5 structure & accessible landmarks
  • Mobile‑first responsive CSS with fluid typography
  • Lightweight vanilla JavaScript — no unnecessary frameworks
  • SEO‑friendly URLs, breadcrumbs & canonical logic
  • Open Graph, Twitter cards and JSON‑LD schema
  • Analytics, search console & conversion event tracking
  • Security headers, HTTPS & spam‑protected forms
  • Easy‑to‑manage CMS so your team can publish freely

We also design for accessibility — proper contrast, keyboard navigation, alt text and readable font sizes. It is good ethics, better UX, and increasingly a ranking and legal consideration.

5 Core Web Vitals & site speed

Speed is both a ranking signal and a revenue signal. Users leave slow sites, and Google measures exactly that behaviour through three Core Web Vitals:

LCP — Largest Contentful Paint

  • Target: under 2.5 seconds
  • Optimise hero images, caching & server response

INP / FID — Interactivity

  • Target: under 200 ms
  • Reduce heavy JavaScript & blocking scripts

CLS — Visual Stability

  • Target: under 0.1
  • Reserve space for images, ads & embeds

Our speed toolkit

  • Modern image formats & lazy loading
  • Minification, compression & CDN delivery

6 Conversion optimisation: traffic means nothing alone

Ranking #1 for a keyword that never buys anything is a vanity metric. Every project includes CRO thinking: clear value proposition above the fold, single focused call‑to‑action, social proof, fast forms, trust signals and mobile thumb‑friendly design. We then test, measure and iterate using real user behaviour instead of opinions.

7 Maintenance, reporting & long‑term growth

Search algorithms change, competitors publish, plugins update and markets shift. Ongoing maintenance is not optional — it protects the investment you already made. Our retainers include technical monitoring, content refreshes, link acquisition, performance checks and a plain‑English monthly report showing rankings, traffic, leads and revenue impact.
